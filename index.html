A little about me.

I'm Daryn, a Design Engineer with a decade of experience in web design and development, and a year specialising in UX. I work across the full process: research, strategy, design, and build, creating web experiences that are as considered under the hood as they look on screen.

I enjoy creating, collaborating, and learning, whether that's exploring new design trends, experimenting with AI tooling, or diving into something completely unrelated. Outside of work you'll find me making music, practicing jiu-jitsu, cooking, gardening, or getting lost in a good film or podcast.
